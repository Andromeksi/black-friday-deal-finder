How the prices were checked

Every deal on this page was found and checked on 27 November 2026. The price was read at the store named on the deal, or in a deals roundup that names that store — the source is printed on each card. The “usual price” is the store’s list price or the price the item had been selling at; the discount is worked out from the two prices, so the numbers always add up.

The verdict is the honest part. A new low means no source has recorded the item cheaper. A strong deal is a big, genuine cut. A routine deal is real but small — or the item has been cheaper before, and the card says so. Two deals here are routine on purpose: a finder that calls everything a bargain is no use to anyone.

Prices move during the sale and stock runs out. Check the price on the store page before you buy — each card links to the store’s own search for that exact product. This site is not affiliated with any store, takes no commission, and loads nothing from the network: the deals live in its own files.
